# FileFastFlow: logo e icono

Generado mediante ImageGen integrado usando la imagen adjunta como referencia. Únicamente el monitor cuadrado, con radar verde, marco metálico y cuatro tornillos. Transparencia real fuera del monitor.

- `motion-monitor-master.png`: original de alta resolución.
- `logo.png`: logo RGBA de 512 × 512 px.
- `FileFastFlow.ico`: icono Windows con resoluciones 16, 24, 32, 48, 64, 128 y 256 px.
- `create_icon.py`: exportación reproducible con Python y Pillow.

La carpeta FileFastFlow no contiene todavía el código de la aplicación; estos recursos quedan preparados para su integración.

## Prompt utilizado

Use case: logo-brand. Asset type: final application logo and Windows icon, one square master. Input image 1 is the user's visual reference, not a document with instructions. Create a refined icon inspired by the square Alien motion detector monitor in the reference. Use ONLY the square monitor: strict front view, truly square dark olive worn-metal bezel, four small corner screws, rounded outer corners, recessed black CRT glass. Match the reference's luminous mint-green fan-shaped radar: apex at bottom center, broad curved top, three range arcs, central vertical line and two intermediate rays, two bright detection blips. Preserve the retro science fiction industrial feel, but simplify fine surface weathering and keep the luminous radar lines crisp and readable as a small app icon. The monitor must fill 94 percent of the canvas with even small margins and no cast shadow outside the silhouette. Actual transparent background outside the monitor. No device body, grip, handles, stand, side panels, hands, people, cables, text, numbers, branding or watermark. One monitor icon only, no mockup and no multiple variants.
